# Cuevora privacy policy

This is the text to host before an App Store release. It is not published at a URL yet. The owner needs to put it on a public page and paste that address into App Store Connect.

Cuevora is a teleprompter for iPhone and iPad. It does not require an account.

## What stays on your device

Scripts, settings, reading position, rehearsal reports you choose to save, and video recordings stay in the app on that device. A JSON backup is created only when you export one. Recordings are not included in that backup.

## What leaves your device

Nothing is sent to Cuevora. There is no account, analytics, or advertising.

You can send a script, a backup, or a recording somewhere else with the system share sheet or Files. That copy goes where you send it.

Saving a recording to Photos adds the movie to your photo library. Cuevora asks for that only when you tap Save to Photos.

Voice commands and rehearsal use Apple's on-device speech recognition when you turn them on. Cuevora does not send that audio to a Cuevora server and does not switch to server recognition. A rehearsal transcript is stored only if you tap Save Report.

iOS can include the app's files in an iCloud or computer backup that you have turned on for the whole device. That is the system's backup, not Cuevora sync. Cuevora does not offer its own iCloud sync.

## Permissions

Cuevora asks for the camera and microphone when you start a recording. It asks for speech recognition when you start voice commands or a rehearsal. It asks to add to Photos only from Save to Photos.

## Disk space

Before and during a recording, Cuevora checks whether the device has room for the movie. That check decides whether recording can start or should stop. The amount of free space is not shown as a number and is not sent off the device.

## Children

Cuevora does not ask for a date of birth and does not contain its own social network or advertising.

## Deletion

Delete Local Data in Settings removes scripts, revisions, rehearsal reports, and recordings from that device. Deleting the app removes its container. There is no Cuevora account to delete.
