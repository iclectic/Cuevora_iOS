# Cuevora support

This is the text to host before an App Store release. It is not published at a URL yet.

Cuevora is a teleprompter. Scripts and recordings stay on the iPhone or iPad unless you export or share them. There is no account to sign in to.

## Common questions

**The camera or microphone was denied.** Open Settings, find Cuevora, and allow Camera and Microphone. Nothing is recorded while access is off.

**Speech commands do nothing.** Turn Voice Commands on in Cuevora settings. The device must support on-device recognition for its language. Cuevora does not send audio to a server. After a recording, or after leaving the app, turn voice commands on again.

**A recording is missing from a backup.** JSON backups leave video out on purpose. The movie stays in Recordings on the device. Use Share or Save to Photos for a copy.

**Delete everything on this device.** Settings, Delete Local Data. Export a backup first if you want the scripts.
